1. INTRODUCTION

Victory Entrepreneurship Centre requires a simple and organised digital platform to manage its training programmes, learners, instructors, learning materials, assignments, assessments and general training activities.

The proposed solution is a custom-built Training Management Platform designed to make it easier for the Centre to manage its training operations while giving learners and instructors a central place to access relevant programme information and carry out their responsibilities.

The platform will provide an organised digital environment where administrators can manage learners and training batches, instructors can communicate with assigned learners and assess their work, and learners can monitor their training activities and progress.

The solution will also provide a public-facing website where prospective learners can learn about the Centre and register or apply for available training programmes.

Virtual classes will be delivered through YouTube Live and OBS Studio, with the relevant live-stream and replay links made available to learners through the platform.

2. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The main objectives of the proposed platform are to:

Provide a central system for managing the Centre's training programmes and learners.

Simplify learner registration, application review and approval.

Organise learners into training programmes and batches.

Enable instructors to manage assigned batches and communicate with learners.

Provide a central location for learning materials, announcements and instructions.

Allow learners to submit assignments digitally where applicable.

Track paper-based assignment submissions and their status.

Enable instructors to record evaluations, scores, feedback and learner progress.

Give learners access to their training information through a personal dashboard.

Provide administrators with an overview of training activities and basic reports.

3. PROPOSED PLATFORM FEATURES

3.1 Public Website and Learner Registration

The platform will include a public-facing website that introduces Victory Entrepreneurship Centre and provides information about its training programmes.

Key features include:

Centre information and programme descriptions.

Display of available training programmes.

Learner registration and application forms.

Collection of relevant learner information.

Submission confirmation.

Administrative access to submitted applications.

Prospective learners will be able to visit the website, review available programmes and submit their registration or application details.

3.2 Learner Management

The platform will provide an organised system for managing registered learners.

Key features include:

Learner profiles.

Learner information and registration records.

Application review and approval by administrators.

Assignment of approved learners to programmes and batches.

Access to individual learner records.

Monitoring of learner participation and progress.

Administrators will be able to review applications before approving learners for the appropriate training programme.

3.3 Programme and Batch Management

Administrators will be able to create and manage training programmes and organise learners into batches.

Key features include:

Creation and management of training programmes.

Creation of training batches.

Assignment of learners to batches.

Management of batch information.

Assignment of instructors to batches.

Access to relevant batch activities and records.

This will help the Centre organise different training sessions and manage learners according to their respective programmes.

3.4 Instructor Management and Communication

The platform will provide instructors with access to their assigned batches and the tools required to communicate with learners.

Key features include:

Instructor profiles.

Assignment of instructors to training batches.

Access to assigned learners.

Posting of batch announcements.

Sharing of instructions and learning updates.

Communication with learners through the platform.

Ability for learners to ask questions or respond to relevant posts.

This will provide a central communication point for instructors and their assigned learners.

3.5 Learning Materials and Resources

Instructors and administrators will be able to share relevant learning resources with learners.

Key features include:

Uploading and sharing learning materials.

Access to programme-specific resources.

Sharing of documents and other learning files.

Organising resources according to training programmes or batches.

Allowing learners to access relevant materials from their dashboards.

This will reduce the need to distribute learning materials through multiple disconnected channels.

3.6 Assignment Management

The platform will include an assignment management feature that allows instructors to create assignments and monitor learner submissions.

Key features include:

Creation of assignments.

Assignment descriptions and instructions.

Assignment deadlines.

Digital assignment submission by learners.

Submission status tracking.

Instructor access to submitted work.

Feedback and evaluation records.

For assignments completed on paper, instructors will be able to record whether the learner has submitted the physical work and update its submission status within the platform.

This means both digital and paper-based assignments can be monitored through one system.

3.7 Evaluation and Learner Progress Tracking

Instructors will be able to record learner evaluations and monitor individual progress.

Key features include:

Recording of assessment scores.

Instructor feedback.

Evaluation records.

Tracking of assignment performance.

Monitoring of learner progress.

Access to evaluation information by authorised users.

Learners will be able to view the results and feedback made available to them.

3.8 Learner Dashboard

Each learner will have access to a personal dashboard containing information relevant to their training programme.

The dashboard will provide access to:

Assigned training programme and batch.

Announcements and instructions.

Learning materials and resources.

Assignment details and deadlines.

Assignment submission status.

Evaluation results and instructor feedback.

Live class links.

Recorded class or replay links.

The dashboard will help learners keep track of their training activities without having to search through multiple communication channels.

3.9 Administrator Dashboard and Basic Reports

Administrators will have a central dashboard for managing the Centre's training activities.

Key features include:

Overview of registered learners.

Application review and approval.

Programme and batch management.

Instructor assignment.

Learner and instructor records.

Assignment and evaluation monitoring.

Basic training activity reports.

The administrative dashboard will provide a convenient overview of the Centre's operations.

3.10 Virtual Class and Replay Access

The platform will support access to virtual training sessions through externally hosted YouTube Live streams.

Key features include:

Display of scheduled live class information.

Placement of live-stream links within the relevant learner dashboard.

Access to recorded class or replay links.

Organisation of class links according to programmes or batches.

The platform will manage access to the relevant class information and links. Actual video broadcasting and live-stream operations will be handled separately using YouTube Live and OBS Studio.

4. VIRTUAL CLASS STREAMING ARRANGEMENT

Virtual classes will be delivered using YouTube Live and OBS Studio.

YouTube Live will serve as the video streaming platform, while OBS Studio will be used to manage the broadcast, including video sources, screen sharing, presentation materials and audio.

The proposed arrangement will allow the Centre to broadcast training sessions and make the relevant live and replay links available to learners through the Training Management Platform.

The streaming setup and live-day technical support are separate services and are not included in the ₦650,000 platform development quotation.

4.1 Streaming Setup Service

The one-time streaming setup service will cover:

YouTube channel readiness and streaming configuration.

OBS Studio installation and configuration.

Setup of the required broadcast scenes and sources.

Configuration of audio and presentation sharing.

Test broadcasts.

Testing with lecturers or instructors.

Preparation of a basic moderator guide.

Training on how to operate the streaming setup.

Preparation of a backup streaming plan.

One-time streaming setup fee: $500

4.2 Live-Day Technical Support

Technical support can be provided during scheduled virtual lectures to help ensure that broadcasts run smoothly.

This service includes:

Pre-session technical checks.

Assistance with OBS Studio and broadcast configuration.

Monitoring and troubleshooting during the lecture.

Support with streaming interruptions where possible.

Assistance with ending and managing the broadcast.
